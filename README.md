<section id="advocacy">
  <h2>ICT as a Medium for Advocacy</h2>
  <h3>What is Advocacy?</h3>
  <p>Advocacy is a process of supporting and enabling people to express their views and concerns, access information and services, and defend and promote their rights and responsibilities.</p>

  <h3>Change.org</h3>
  <p>Change.org is an online platform where people can create petitions and ask others to sign them. Digital signatures allow online communities to support causes and campaigns.</p>
  <p>Examples of advocacy topics include economic problems, criminal injustice, human rights, environmental concerns, animal welfare, and public health.</p>

  <h3>People Finder and Online Communities</h3>
  <p>Online tools can also help people locate relatives and share information during calamities. Social networks and petition platforms can help communities organize and communicate.</p>

  <h3>Million People March</h3>
  <p>The Million People March was a series of protests held in the Philippines in August 2013. Social media and online platforms were among the communication tools used to spread information about the movement.</p>
</section>

<section id="change">
  <h2>ICT as a Platform for Change</h2>
  <h3>EDSA People Power Revolution</h3>
  <p>The EDSA People Power Revolution was a major protest movement in the Philippines in February 1986. It demonstrated how communication and collective action can contribute to social and political change.</p>

  <h3>EDSA Dos / 2001</h3>
  <p>The 2001 EDSA uprising demonstrated the use of mobile communication and text messages to organize and mobilize people.</p>

  <div class="highlight"><strong>Key idea:</strong> ICT can support communication, organization, awareness, advocacy, and participation in social change.</div>
</section>

<section id="youtube">
  <h2>Inserting YouTube Videos on a Blog Post</h2>
  <ol>
    <li>Search for an OPM song or other appropriate video on YouTube.</li>
    <li>Go to the bottom of the video and click <strong>Share</strong>.</li>
    <li>Click <strong>Embed</strong>.</li>
    <li>Copy the embed code.</li>
    <li>Paste the code into the appropriate editor or HTML/text tab of your website.</li>
    <li>Preview the page, then update or publish the content.</li>
  </ol>
  <div class="highlight">Tip: To add your own YouTube video, replace the example embed with the video's official YouTube embed code.</div>
</section>
